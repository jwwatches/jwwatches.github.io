Frequently Asked Questions

Are your watch recommendations paid for? +
Never. Our recommendations are based purely on quality, value, and relevance to the category. We may earn affiliate commissions when you buy through our links, but this has no influence on which watches we feature or how we rank them.
How do you choose which watches to feature? +
We select watches based on their quality, value for money, relevance to the category, and real-world reputation. We cover a genuine range of budgets and brands — from Casio to Patek Philippe — because great watches exist at every price point.
Can you help me find a watch for my budget? +
Absolutely. Use our Price Range guides to browse by budget, or send us a message with your budget and what you're looking for — we're happy to give a personal recommendation.
I found an error in your specs. How do I report it? +
We take accuracy seriously and appreciate corrections. Use the contact form above, select "Correction / Inaccuracy" as the subject, and tell us which watch and spec is wrong. We'll verify and update it promptly.
