Halloween Watches: Blood Moons, Pumpkin Dials & Shadow-Ready Timepieces

You don't need a costume to dress for the season. From eerie moonphase complications that track the actual lunar cycle to pumpkin-orange dials built for the darkest nights of the year, here are eight watches with just enough spook to earn a spot on your wrist this October.

Blood Moon Moonphase Watches

A moonphase complication is the one piece of watchmaking wizardry that's genuinely eerie — a tiny mechanical sky tracking the same lunar cycle that's supposedly responsible for werewolves and bad luck. These four wear that theme without ever feeling like a costume piece.

01

~$450–550
Eco-Drive Moonphase Radio-Controlled

Named for the Shinto moon deity, the Tsuki-yomi renders its phase indicator as a slow-turning starfield rather than a cartoon crescent — genuinely one of the more atmospheric moonphase displays at this price, and it never needs a battery change.

Swiss Made Square Case Automatic

A square case is already an unusual silhouette for a dress watch; paired with a deep midnight-blue moonphase aperture it reads closer to art deco gothic than anything costume-y. This is the pick for someone who wants the theme dialed down to a whisper.

The entry point into a "real" moonphase, and the dark charcoal dial version leans into the season better than the silver — the moon disc sits in near-black shadow until it catches the light at an angle.
