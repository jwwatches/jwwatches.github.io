The Best Tudor Watches for the Money

Tudor is what happens when Rolex's sister company decides to actually compete on value. Same in-house movement quality trickling down, a fraction of the wait, and — on a few key references — real availability through Amazon. Here are the seven worth your money.

01

Black Bay 58 (M79030N)

$3,800–$4,200
39mmIn-House MovementMost Requested Tudor

Named for Tudor's first-ever dive watch from 1958, this is the reference that turned Tudor from "Rolex's little brother" into a destination brand in its own right. The 39mm case solved the one complaint everyone had about modern dive watches — that they'd all gotten too big.
