12 Essential Blue Dial Watches

Blue dials dominate the watch world — from casual divers to the most coveted timepieces ever made. The Nautilus made blue iconic. Everything else followed.

Prices are approximate and change frequently. Always check Amazon for current pricing.

Blue is the most coveted dial color in watchmaking — and for good reason. From the deep navy of a Tudor Black Bay to the electric cobalt of an Omega Seamaster, blue dials have a way of catching light that no other color can match. Watch collectors obsess over blue because it manages to be both versatile and expressive: it works with a suit, looks at home at the beach, and somehow gets better with every glance. The shade matters enormously — icy blue reads as sporty, deep navy as classic, sunburst blue as luxurious. What you'll find here spans $40 to $120,000+, but the obsession is the same at every price point: that perfect, impossible blue.
