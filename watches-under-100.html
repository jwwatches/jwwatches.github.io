Casio, Timex, Invicta, and Rdunae — sixteen picks that do exactly what a watch should do, without asking you to apologize for the price tag.

The under-$100 category gets dismissed by watch snobs and loved by everyone else. Casio builds dive watches here that cost more to service than to buy. Timex has been making reliable daily wearers since before your parents were born. The F-91W is worn by presidents, astronauts, and people who simply don't care what you think about their watch. This list doesn't apologize for a single entry.
